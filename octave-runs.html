Octave Runs

One octave, root to root. Start on each note of the scale and you get a different shape — seven in all. Every one fits under the hand in four or five frets and crosses exactly three strings.

No runs starting on the B or high E string: an octave needs eight notes, and with only two strings left you'd need four on one of them — four scale notes in a row always span at least five frets, which is past the hand.
