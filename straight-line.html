There Are No Straight Lines

You were taught to put a quantity on an x-y graph and extend it with a ruler. A line drawn that way has no end — it cannot have one, because a line is the one shape with nothing to run out of.
A sphere is always at some size. Big or small, it is somewhere, and it can stop, and it can come back. Here is the same set of numbers held both ways: once as the line you were taught to draw, and once as a thing with a size.
after Hans Rosling, Factfulness — the straight line instinct
