Blockout · 50 places · feet

One walk, fifty places, five blocks of ten

Buildings on your left the whole way, an open edge on your right. One side only, so "the fourth building" has exactly one meaning — and a cross street between each block, which is what makes ten feel like ten.

Main Street1–10 · shops
Civic Square11–20 · institutions
The Works21–30 · industry
The Park31–40 · green
The Waterfront41–50 · water

Five vocabularies instead of one, because fifty shops blur and a shop and a lighthouse never do. Each block has one landmark tall enough to see from two blocks off — cinema sign, church spire, water tower, observatory dome, lighthouse.

Press O for silhouette mode. That's the production test: if two places have the same outline they are the same place, however different they look. Every one of these is a placeholder to be replaced with a real model — the shapes exist only to be told apart.
