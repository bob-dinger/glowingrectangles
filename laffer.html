Which Side Of The Hill

Everyone agrees the Laffer curve exists. Both ends are arithmetic: at 0% you collect nothing, at 100% nobody earns anything to collect. The whole fight is one number — how sharply people pull back when you tax them.
τ* = 1 / (1 + a·e) Diamond & Saez (2011). a = 1.5, the Pareto parameter. e is the elasticity of taxable income — the contested one.
