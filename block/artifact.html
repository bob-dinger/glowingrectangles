Blockout · everything in feet

Four lots, two streets

Four 50 × 50 ft lots in a 2 × 2 grid, split by two 20 ft streets crossing at the centre. Site total is 120 × 120 ft.

The grey masses are placeholders, not buildings — turn them off with Buildings. Sidewalks are cut from the lots rather than the roadway, so the streets stay exactly 20 ft as specified.

Every dimension comes from six numbers at the top of src/main.js — LOT, STREET, COLS, ROWS, WALK, PAD. Change one and the whole block rebuilds, so going more detailed is cheap.
