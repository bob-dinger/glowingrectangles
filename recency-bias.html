What is Recency Bias?

We give disproportionate weight to recent events, even when older data is more representative.

The last thing we experienced feels more important and more likely to happen again.

"What happened last is what will happen next."

In This Demo

Watch as circles pass by. Most are blue, but when a gold one appears, it dominates the observer's memory.

Even after seeing many blue circles, one recent gold makes them think "it's all gold!"

Real World Examples

Stock Market Decisions
After a good week, we expect more gains. After a bad week, we panic sell. Both ignore long-term trends.
Sports "Hot Streaks"
A player makes 3 shots in a row and we think they're "hot." Statistics show the next shot is no more likely to go in.
Performance Reviews
Managers remember the last month most clearly, even when reviewing a full year of work.
Weather Predictions
After a week of rain, we pack umbrellas for vacation—even when forecasts say sun.
