This building is generated from plain text. Hashes are walls, plus signs are doorways, letters are room floors — one character per 0.75 metres.

Open Edit plan text, change a character, and the building rebuilds as you type. That's the whole point: you can redraw a room in a text box, and so can a program — no modelling tool in between.

Compare the Museum and the House. Same ten rooms, same footprint. The museum is a chain, so reaching room 7 requires passing through 6 — the building enforces the order. The house hangs every room off a corridor, which is nicer to live in and worse to remember.
