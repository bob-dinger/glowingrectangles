The insight

Negative isn't scary or weird.

It just means left instead of right.

-3 = 3 steps left from zero
+3 = 3 steps right from zero

Same distance. Opposite direction.

Real life

Temperature: 10° below zero = -10°

Money: $5 in debt = -$5

Floors: 2 below ground = -2
