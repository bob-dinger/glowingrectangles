Multiplication Methods

Same answer. Different paths. Find what clicks for you.

There's no single "right" way to multiply. Different cultures developed different techniques. Some are visual. Some are fast. Some work better for certain numbers.

Explore them all. See which one makes sense to YOUR brain.

Japan / China
Line Method
Draw intersecting lines, count where they cross. Pure visual—no memorization needed.
Best for: small digits (1s, 2s, 3s)
India (Vedic)
Nikhilam
For numbers near 10, 100, 1000. Uses deviations from the base. Feels like magic.
Best for: 97×96, 102×108, etc.
India (Vedic)
Urdhva Tiryak
"Vertically and crosswise." A systematic pattern that works for any numbers.
Best for: general use
Arabic / Medieval Europe
Lattice Method
Grid with diagonals. Multiply in boxes, add along the diagonals. Hard to mess up.
Best for: big numbers, avoiding carry mistakes
Visual / Modern
Area Model
Break numbers into parts, draw rectangles. See multiplication AS area.
Best for: understanding what multiplication means
Russia / Ancient
Russian Peasant
Halve one number, double the other, add the right rows. Weird but works.
Best for: when you only know how to double
Ancient Egypt
Egyptian Doubling
Build up through doubling. The ancestor of binary multiplication.
Best for: understanding how computers multiply
