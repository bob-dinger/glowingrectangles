y = mx + b

The equation IS the picture. The picture IS the equation.

y = 2x + 1
m = slope (steepness) 2
m is how steep the line is.
Positive = going up. Negative = going down.
m = 2 means: go right 1, go up 2.
b = y-intercept (starting point) 1
b is where the line crosses the y-axis.
It's where you "start" when x = 0.

The connection

Every equation y = mx + b draws exactly one line.

Every line can be written as exactly one equation.

Algebra and geometry are the same thing in different languages.
