How it works

For 2-digit × 2-digit:

1. Multiply ones vertically
2. Cross-multiply & add
3. Multiply tens vertically

Why it's powerful

This pattern extends to ANY size numbers. 3-digit, 4-digit, anything.

Once you learn the pattern, you can multiply huge numbers left-to-right!
