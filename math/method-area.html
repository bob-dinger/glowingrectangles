The core idea

23 × 14 is the same as asking:

"What's the area of a 23 × 14 rectangle?"

Break the rectangle into smaller pieces. Add them up.

Why it works

This is the distributive property made visible:

23 × 14 = (20+3) × (10+4)

= 20×10 + 20×4 + 3×10 + 3×4

Each box is one of those pieces!
