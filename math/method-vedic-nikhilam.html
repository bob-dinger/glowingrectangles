(base - a) × (base - b)
= (base - a - b) × base + (a × b)

When to use it

Numbers close to a power of 10:

• 97, 98, 99 (near 100)
• 996, 997 (near 1000)
• 102, 103 (near 100)

The closer to the base, the easier!

Why it works

It's algebra in disguise:

97 × 94 = (100-3)(100-6)
= 10000 - 300 - 600 + 18
= 10000 - 900 + 18
= 9118
