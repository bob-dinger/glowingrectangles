Place Value

Each position is worth 10× more than the one to its right

1234
Number (0-9999)

The pattern

Each place is 10× the previous:

Ones × 10 = Tens
Tens × 10 = Hundreds
Hundreds × 10 = Thousands

Place values

Thousands × 1000
Hundreds × 100
Tens × 10
Ones × 1

Base 10

We use 10 digits (0-9).

When we run out at 9, we "carry" to the next place.

9 + 1 = 10
(0 ones, 1 ten)
