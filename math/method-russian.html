How it works

1. Write both numbers
2. Halve the left (drop remainders)
3. Double the right
4. Repeat until left = 1
5. Cross out rows where left is EVEN
6. Add the remaining right values

Why it works

It's binary multiplication in disguise!

Odd numbers in the left column = 1s in binary. You're adding powers of 2 × the other number.

This is how computers multiply.
