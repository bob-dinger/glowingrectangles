Trig is Circles

Sine and cosine are just shadows of a point going around

Angle θ 0°
sin(θ) 0.00
cos(θ) 1.00
Speed

What you're seeing

A point moves around a circle.

sin(θ) = the point's height (y)
cos(θ) = the point's horizontal position (x)

The waves on the right? That's just the height over time. That's ALL trig is.
