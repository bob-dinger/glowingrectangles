The Secret

You memorized up to 12 × 12 = 144. But what about 13 × 7? Or 23 × 15?

The tables stop. The method doesn't.

The real skill

Any multiplication is just smaller multiplications added together. Break numbers into 10s, 5s, and 1s—pieces you already know.

7 × 12 becomes:
7 × 10 = 70
7 × 2 = 14
70 + 14 = 84

This is called the distributive property, but that name was hidden from you until algebra—as if it were something different. It's not. It's just breaking things into pieces.
