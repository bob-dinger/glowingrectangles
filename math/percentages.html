Percentage

The word itself

Per = for each
Cent = hundred

"Per cent" literally means "for each hundred"

25% = 25 per 100 = 25/100

Real world

Half 50%
Quarter 25%
Three quarters 75%
All of it 100%
None of it 0%

Converting

% → decimal: divide by 100
25% = 25 ÷ 100 = 0.25

decimal → %: multiply by 100
0.25 × 100 = 25%
