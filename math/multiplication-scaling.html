Multiplication is Scaling

Stretching and shrinking numbers on a line

Start with 4
Scale by 1
Result 4
4 × 1 = 4

The Idea

You were probably taught that 3 × 4 means "add 4 three times." That works for whole numbers, but it's a limited view.

Multiplication is really about scaling—stretching or shrinking a number along a line.

Why this matters

"Repeated addition" can't explain multiplying by 0.5 (half of something) or by -1 (flip direction). Scaling explains all of it.

When you multiply by 2, the number stretches to twice its distance from zero.

Multiply by 0.5, and it shrinks to half.

Multiply by -1, and it flips across zero—same distance, opposite direction.

Try this

  • Set multiplier to 1 (nothing changes)
  • Slide to 2, then 3 (stretching)
  • Slide to 0.5 (shrinking)
  • Slide to 0 (everything becomes 0)
  • Slide to -1 (flip across zero)
  • Try a negative base number
