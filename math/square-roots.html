What number times itself makes this?

Squaring
52 = 25
Square Root
√25 = 5
A 5 × 5 square has 25 blocks
5 blocks wide × 5 blocks tall
Square asks
5 × 5 = ?
"What's the area?"
Square root asks
? × ? = 25
"What's the side length?"
√25 = 5 because 5 × 5 = 25
Square root finds the side length when you know the area

The inverse

Square root: I know the area. What's the side?

They undo each other!

Why "square"?

Because 5 × 5 literally makes a square shape!

The name comes from geometry. It's counting how many unit squares fit.
