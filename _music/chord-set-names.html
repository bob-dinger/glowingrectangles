Every Chord Set

Six chords live in a major key once you drop the diminished seventh, so there are exactly 20 sets of three and 15 sets of four — and that is all of them. Each is named for the most famous song whose section uses exactly that palette, and the number is how many songs in the corpus do. Order is not part of the identity: C G Am F and C F G Am are the same set.

Read down either column and the colour drifts. Every set in the top half holds the tonic and at least one pillar; the rare ones at the bottom are the ones where C goes missing. Popularity here is almost entirely “does it contain home”.

Dm Em Am is exactly the three minor chords — and it is the rarest set of three, at six songs. Add one chord to it and you get three of the fours: add F for September, add G for And I Love Her, add C for Shake Me Down.

C Em G is the tonic triad with each note harmonised — the notes of a C chord, turned back into chords.
