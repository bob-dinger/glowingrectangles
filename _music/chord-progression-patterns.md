# Chord Progression Patterns

Common structural patterns in 8, 12, 16, and 18 measure sections.

---

## AABA (The Standard)

The classic form. Two statements, contrast, return.

### Eight Days a Week (Verse - 16 measures, D major)
```
A: D    | E/A  | G    | D     (4 bars)
A: D    | E/A  | G    | D     (4 bars)
B: Bm   | Em   | Bm   | E/A   (4 bars)
A: D    | E/A  | G    | D     (4 bars)
```
- A sections: I - II/5 - IV - I (the "Eight Days" sound)
- B section: vi - ii - vi - II/5 (relative minor territory)
- Final A is identical (or slight variation Aʹ)

---

## AABAʹ (Standard with Closing Variation)

Same as AABA but the final A has a twist to conclude.

### I'm Looking Through You (Verse - 18 measures, G major)
```
A: G  | Am7 | Em | D    (4 bars)
A: G  | Am7 | Em | D    (4 bars)
B: Em | Am  | G  | D    (4 bars)
Aʹ: G | Am  | C  | G | G | G  (6 bars - extended ending!)
```
- A sections: I - ii7 - vi - V
- B section: vi - ii - I - V (starts on vi, contrast)
- Aʹ: Extended to 6 bars with C (IV) and repeated I to close

---

## ABAC (The Surprise)

Return home, then somewhere NEW. The C is the payoff.

### Norwegian Wood (Bridge - 8 measures, D major)
```
A: Dm   | Dm           (2 bars)
B: G    | G            (2 bars)
A: Dm   | Dm           (2 bars)
C: Em   | A            (2 bars - two chords! acceleration)
```
- A sections: borrowed iv (Dm in D major - modal mixture)
- B section: IV
- C section: ii - V (the turnaround) — TWO chords in 2 bars = acceleration
- **Key pattern: C section shortens harmonic rhythm to accelerate into resolution**

---

## ABAC with Harmonic Acceleration

Same as ABAC but the C phrase packs more chords in to push forward.

### Don't Look Back in Anger (Verse - 8 measures, C major)
```
A: C  | G            (2 bars - 1 chord per bar)
B: Am | E/G          (2 bars - 1 chord per bar)
A: F  | G            (2 bars - 1 chord per bar)
C: C  | Am→G         (2 bars - but Am is 1.5 beats, G is 2.5 beats!)
```
- A and B sections: steady 1 chord per bar
- C section: **acceleration** — Am-G crammed into final bar
- Same pattern as Norwegian Wood bridge
- Creates momentum pushing into next section

## Key Observations

### Harmonic Acceleration
Many patterns end with faster chord changes in the final phrase:
- Norwegian Wood bridge: 1 chord per 2 bars → 2 chords in final 2 bars (Em-A)
- Don't Look Back in Anger verse: 1 chord per bar → 2 chords in final bar (Am-G)
- Help! verse: same pattern

This is a **major pattern** — the C phrase in ABAC often accelerates to create momentum.

### The Aʹ Variation
The final A almost never exact:
- Extended (I'm Looking Through You: 4 bars → 6 bars)
- Different ending chord
- Added tag or turnaround
- Melodic variation while chords stay same

### Phrase Lengths
- 8 measures = 4 × 2-bar phrases (most common)
- 12 measures = 4 × 3-bar or 6 × 2-bar
- 16 measures = 8 × 2-bar or 4 × 4-bar
- 18 measures = irregular (often 4+4+4+6 with extended ending)
