# One image for all fifteen four-chord groups

Fifteen groups, fifteen things in a single scene. Each word spells its chord group by
its consonant sounds (k/g=C, t/d=Dm, m=Em, f/v=F, j/sh/ch=G, s/z=Am).

## The prompt

> A wide, richly detailed storybook illustration of a riverbank fairground below a stone
> castle at golden hour, painted in warm gouache with clear, readable staging — every
> element distinct and separated, nothing overlapping.
>
> **In the river, foreground left:** a huge whiskered **catfish** breaking the surface, and
> beside it an **anglerfish** with an enormous toothy mouth and a glowing lure on its head.
>
> **On the left bank:** a **foxhound** with its nose down in the reeds, tail up. Behind it,
> a soldier in leafy **camouflage** crouched in the bushes, only his painted face showing.
>
> **Centre, at a stone table:** a **queen in a crown and heavy robes** plays chess against a
> **ragged misfit** in mismatched clothes and one missing shoe. On the board, the black king
> lies toppled — **checkmate**. A **giant furry sports mascot** in a costume leans over the
> table with both arms raised, cheering. Next to them a man stands utterly baffled, hands
> clutching his head.
>
> **On the right:** a **tribal chief** in a tall feathered headdress seated on a carved
> wooden throne. Small **imps stealing chess pieces** and scampering off. A **street preacher**
> standing on a crate with both arms flung skyward. A **bagpiper in a kilt and tartan**. And a
> **doctor in a white coat with a stethoscope**, examining a seated patient.
>
> **In the background:** a black **steam locomotive** crossing a tall stone viaduct, trailing
> smoke across the sunset.
>
> Storybook illustration, warm palette, crisp outlines, everything clearly legible.

## Notes

SKIRMISH was the obvious word for C Em G Am and had to go: it contains an **r**, and r is
E major, a chord outside the group. EXAMINATION is the r-free replacement. Five others were
swapped from the sheet for the same reason or for being undrawable — SCAVENGE became
GOOSEFISH, CONGEST became SCOTTISH, FIXED became FOXHOUND, JUSTIFY became EVANGELIST,
COMFORT became LOCOMOTIVE. All fifteen verified against cmudict.

Fifteen distinct subjects is a lot for one generation. If elements start merging or
dropping, the fallback is three images of five — river and bank, the chess table, the right
bank — or a fifteen-panel grid with one vignette each.
