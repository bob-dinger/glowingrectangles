# One image for all fifteen two-chord groups

Fifteen pairs. Each word spells its pair by its consonant sounds
(k/g=C, t/d=Dm, m=Em, f/v=F, j/sh/ch=G, s/z=Am).

**Built as five things HAPPENING, not fifteen things sitting.** Objects in a row are
fifteen separate things to remember; an axe splitting a vat so foam bursts out is one.
Every item here is doing something to another item.

## The prompt

> A wide, richly detailed storybook illustration of a smuggler's camp at a cave mouth
> beside a muddy river, late afternoon, warm gouache, crisp outlines. Five separate
> pieces of action, clearly staged and not overlapping.
>
> **Far left — the axe, the vat, the foam:** a woodsman swings an **axe** and splits a
> huge wooden **vat** clean open; white **foam** bursts out of the crack in a wave across
> the ground.
>
> **Centre left — the cat, the mouse, the gum:** a tabby **cat** mid-pounce, claws out,
> at a small brown **mouse** whose back feet are stuck fast in a stretched pink wad of
> chewing **gum**, the gum pulling into long strings.
>
> **Centre — the jet, the cash, the cave:** a silver **jet** roars low over the dark
> arched **mouth of the cave**, its cargo hatch open, **banknotes** pouring out and
> fluttering down over the rocks.
>
> **Right — the fish, the mud, the dice:** a fat **fish** launches itself out of the
> churned brown **mud** of the riverbank and catches a tumbling white **die** in its mouth,
> a second die still in the air.
>
> **Foreground right — the sage, the jam, the fizz:** a hand drops a sprig of green
> **sage** into an open jar of red **jam**, which erupts in **fizzing bubbles** over the rim.
>
> Storybook illustration, warm late-afternoon palette, everything clearly legible.

## What each thing is

CAT - C Dm - the pouncing cat
GUM - C Em - gum the mouse is stuck in
CAVE - C F - cave mouth the jet flies over
CASH - C G - banknotes pouring from the jet
AXE - C Am - axe splitting the vat
MUD - Dm Em - mud the fish leaps from
VAT - Dm F - vat being split
JET - Dm G - jet dropping the cash
DICE - Dm Am - die the fish catches
FOAM - Em F - foam bursting from the vat
JAM - Em G - jam the sage goes into
MOUSE - Em Am - mouse the cat is pouncing on
FISH - F G - fish leaping from the mud
FIZZ - F Am - fizz erupting from the jam
SAGE - G Am - sage dropped into the jam

## Notes

Verified against cmudict, covering the fifteen pairs exactly, audited clear of r, p, b, th
and w — those sounds are E, A♯ and D, chords outside the pair.

Three words swapped from the sheet for being undrawable: ASK became AXE, SAD became DICE,
SUM became MOUSE. Far fewer than the larger sets need, because two sounds is short enough
that concrete nouns come easily.

Five vignettes of three is also the fallback: if one frame gets crowded, generate them
separately.
