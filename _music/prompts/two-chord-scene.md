# One image for all fifteen two-chord groups

Fifteen pairs, fifteen things in a single scene. Each word spells its pair by its
consonant sounds (k/g=C, t/d=Dm, m=Em, f/v=F, j/sh/ch=G, s/z=Am).

## The prompt

> A wide, richly detailed storybook illustration of a smuggler's camp at a cave mouth
> beside a muddy river, late afternoon, painted in warm gouache with clear readable
> staging — every element distinct and separated, nothing overlapping.
>
> **Centre:** the dark arched **mouth of a cave** in a rock face, timber props at its edge.
>
> **The riverbank, foreground:** thick churned **mud** with bootprints. A **fish** leaping
> from the brown water.
>
> **Left of the cave:** a big wooden **vat** with white **foam** spilling over its rim and
> down the staves. An **axe** buried in a chopping block beside it.
>
> **Right of the cave, on a barrel:** a loose pile of **banknotes** and two white **dice**
> mid-roll. Next to them a jar of red **jam**, an open bottle of soda with **bubbles**
> fizzing out of the neck, a stick of chewing **gum** half-unwrapped, and a sprig of green
> **sage**.
>
> **Low and near:** a tabby **cat** crouched, eyes fixed on a small brown **mouse** by the
> barrel leg.
>
> **Sky:** a silver **jet** crossing high above, leaving a thin contrail.
>
> Storybook illustration, warm late-afternoon palette, crisp outlines, everything clearly
> legible.

## Notes

All fifteen verified against cmudict, covering the fifteen pairs exactly, audited clear of
r, p, b, th and w — those sounds are E, A♯ and D, chords outside the pair.

Only three needed swapping, far fewer than the larger sets: ASK became AXE, SAD became
DICE, SUM became MOUSE. Two sounds is a short enough word that concrete nouns are easy;
it is the three- and four-chord sets that run out of them and drift into abstractions.

Fifteen subjects in one frame is achievable but crowded. Fallback: three images of five —
the cave and vat, the barrel, the river and sky.
