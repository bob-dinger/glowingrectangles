Fifty Chord Groups

Six chords live in a major key once the diminished seventh is set aside, which makes 15 pairs, 20 threes and 15 fours — fifty groups, and that is all of them. Each has a song that is the clearest example of it, and a word that spells it: the six chords are lettered C D E F G A, so DOG is Dm–G, FACE is C–Em–F–Am and CAGE is C–Em–G–Am. Order is not part of the identity.
