Chord Groups, In Sounds

The same fifty groups, spelled the other way. Not by letter — by the first consonant sound of each syllable, using the nine-sound mapping. MAGIC is m–j–k, which is Em–G–C. CATFISH is k–t–f–sh: C–Dm–F–G. The word also hands you an order to play them in, which the letter spellings never did.
k gC t dDm mEm f vF j sh chG s zAm p bA♯ th wD rE n lfree
fifteen pairssongs
twenty threessongs
fifteen fourssongs
Words carrying r, p, b, th or w are excluded — those sounds are E, A♯ and D, so they would smuggle in a chord that is not in the group. n and l are free: no chord uses them.
