# AABA / I-I-IV-I Blues Progression

Two measures on the tonic (I), one measure on the IV, back to the I. This is the foundation of the 12-bar blues but appears constantly in pop/rock in compressed form as a 4-bar phrase.

## Pattern

| A | A | B | A |
| I | I | IV | I |

## Examples

- Get Back (Beatles) — D, D, A, D
- Paperback Writer (Beatles)
- Margaritaville (Jimmy Buffett)
- Have You Ever Seen the Rain (CCR)

## Notes

- Core blues structure: sit on the tonic, move to IV to create tension, return
- The IV chord feels like a "lean" — just enough movement to create interest
- In full 12-bar blues this expands to: I I I I | IV IV I I | V IV I V
- The compressed 4-bar version (I I IV I) is one of the most common phrases in all popular music
