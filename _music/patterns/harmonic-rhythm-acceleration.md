# Harmonic Rhythm Acceleration

The rate of chord changes increases toward the end of a phrase, creating momentum toward the cadence. Pattern is always "long, long... then shorter, shorter" — the harmonic rhythm speeds up as you approach a structural boundary.

## Pattern Shapes

- `1, 1, .5, .5, 1` — A Little Help From My Friends (verse)
- `2, 2, 2, .5, .5, 1` — Help! (verse)
- `2, 2, 2, 1, 1` — Norwegian Wood (bridge)
- Don't Look Back in Anger — end of each verse

## Notes

- One of the most universal patterns in popular music
- Creates a sense of urgency/arrival before the resolution
- The final chord is often back to full duration (landing on the cadence)
- In classical theory: "harmonic rhythm acceleration toward cadence"
