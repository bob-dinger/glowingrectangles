Why These Four?

Most dirt falls into a few categories. These four ingredients cover them all:

💧 Water

Dissolves water-soluble dirt, dust, and fresh spills. The universal solvent.

🧼 Soap

Breaks surface tension so water can penetrate. Lifts grease and oil by surrounding particles.

🍶 Vinegar (Acid)

Dissolves mineral deposits like hard water stains, rust, and soap scum. Kills some bacteria.

�ite Baking Soda (Base)

Neutralizes acids, deodorizes, and provides gentle abrasion for scrubbing.

Never mix vinegar + bleach (toxic chlorine gas) or vinegar + hydrogen peroxide (corrosive acid).

Commercial Cleaners Decoded

Glass Cleaner
= water + vinegar + tiny bit of soap
All-Purpose Cleaner
= water + soap + fragrance
Bathroom Cleaner
= water + acid (vinegar/citric) + soap
Scouring Powder
= baking soda + soap + abrasives
The $3 Cleaning Kit
Spray bottle of water + vinegar (1:1), bar of castile soap, box of baking soda. That's it.
