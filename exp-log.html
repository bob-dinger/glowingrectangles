Exponents & Logarithms

They're the same question asked backwards

Exponent question
23 = ?
2 multiplied by itself 3 times = ?
Logarithm question
log2(8) = ?
How many times do I multiply 2 to get 8?
→
Exponent finds the result
←
Log finds the exponent
Watch it grow: multiply 2 by itself 3 times
23 = 8   ⟷   log2(8) = 3

Same three numbers. Same relationship. Different question.
Base (multiply this)
Exponent (how many times)

The inverse

Exponent: I know the base and power. What's the result?

Logarithm: I know the base and result. What's the power?

They undo each other. Like + and −.

Think of it as

23 = 8 means:
"2 × 2 × 2 = 8"

log₂(8) = 3 means:
"You need 3 twos to make 8"
