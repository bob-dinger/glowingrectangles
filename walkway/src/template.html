The Walkway

This is the exemplar the other four regions copy. Ten stations on a single path, in a fixed order, each one a different kind of place. Learn this shape once and you can re-walk it in a desert, a tundra, or a city — same ten beats, different dressing.

  1. 1The Gate
  2. 2The Great Tree
  3. 3The Well
  4. 4The Bridge
  5. 5The Mill
  6. 6The Cabin
  7. 7The Loft
  8. 8The Barn
  9. 9The Shrine
  10. 10The Overlook

Every peg is hand-sized, at chest height, on a surface you walk up to — the well's rim, the cabin table, the cairn. Nothing to circle, nothing towering over you. Presidents 1–10 are standing in for real content so the scale can be judged honestly.

The map is not a separate screen. Scroll and you rise out of the valley until you can see all ten; scroll back and you settle where you were standing. Click any number to walk to it.
