Ten Thousand A Week

Before one unit shipped, about $100 million had been spent building the Segway, and its inventor was talking about ten thousand a week. Two spheres here: what was believed, and what was bought. Both are units, both are drawn so area is the count.
The belief was not stupid. It was induction — a very big conclusion from very little data, which is the same machinery that gets everything else right. It just ran ahead of the only evidence that settles it, which arrives last: whether anybody buys one.
