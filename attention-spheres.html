Inside the sphere — looking out

You are standing in the middle of it. Everyone around you is turned the same way, toward the same thing. Turn your head and you see them, not it.

Leaving a sphere is not a decision you announce. The view just swings, and you are among different people looking at something else.

Illustrative — the shape of the thing, not a measurement.
