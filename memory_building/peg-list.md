# The peg list

45 people, 47 presidencies. Generated from `palace/src/presidents.js` — if you
change a peg, change it there and regenerate rather than editing this file.

Two systems are mixed deliberately. **Sound-alikes** (Buchanan → cannon,
Coolidge → cool fridge, Biden → bidet) carry the forgettable presidents;
**iconic images** (Lincoln's top hat, Nixon's tapes, T.R.'s teddy bear) carry
the ones who already own a picture. Pure sound-alike gets strained; pure iconic
fails for the mid-century blanks.

Three pairs of related presidents share an **escalating** image, so the pair is
stored as a pair rather than as two unrelated facts:

| First | Escalated |
|---|---|
| 2 Adams — an atom | 6 J.Q. Adams — an atom **bomb** |
| 9 Harrison — a hairy son | 23 B. Harrison — a hairy **grand**son |
| 41 Bush — a shrub | 43 G.W. Bush — a **burning** shrub |

Marked `·twice·`: Cleveland (22, 24) and Trump (45, 47) served non-consecutive
terms. One place each, entered twice.
