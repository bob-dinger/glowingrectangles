Two Ways of Thinking

When you look at a rock, what do you see?

Concrete Thinkers

See the thing itself. The rock is gray, heavy, rough. It's about 6 inches wide. You could use it to prop open a door.

  • Focus on specific details
  • Think in terms of examples
  • Prefer step-by-step instructions
  • Remember facts and events

Abstract Thinkers

See what it represents. The rock is permanence, stability, foundation. It's a symbol of something that endures.

  • Focus on patterns and concepts
  • Think in terms of principles
  • Prefer understanding the "why"
  • Remember themes and meanings
Concrete
"The rock weighs about 2 pounds and has a gray color."
Abstract
"The rock represents the foundation upon which we build."
Neither is better. Concrete thinking excels at practical tasks and precise communication. Abstract thinking excels at finding patterns and transferring knowledge across domains.

Most people blend both styles, but tend to favor one. Understanding your tendency helps you communicate better with those who think differently.
