In 1950 the surface climbs steeply from front to back — suicide was something that happened to the old. By 2018 the middle of the range is almost level. The ramp became a plateau.

The far ridge never goes away. Men over 85 are still the highest of any group, at 47.2.

Crude rates, males, all races. Years are spaced by their real distance, so the 1950–1980 gap is wide: CDC published decade snapshots until 1981 and annual figures after.

Source: CDC/NCHS, Health United States, suicide by sex and age.
